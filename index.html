History: key events of each period

  1. 1956, 1957, 1958 John Lennon, aged 16, forms the Quarrymen; Paul joins, aged 15; George joins, aged 15
  2. 1960 Stuart Sutcliffe (bass) joins, name change to the Silver Beatles; Pete Best (drums) joins, name change to the Beatles , first trip playing in Hamburg
  3. 1961, 1962 Stuart quits, Brian Epstein becomes manager; Pete Best is replaced with Ringo Starr, "Love Me Do" hits UK charts
  4. 1963: Beatlemania begins Please Please Me is released and popularizes the Beatles through Europe, they perform for the Royals, their first two albums dominate the charts, and "I Want to Hold Your Hand" tops U.S. charts by year's end
  5. 1964 The Beatles tour the U.S. to riotous response, and release the film A Hard Day's Night
  6. 1965, 1966 The second film Help! premieres, and the Beatles begin toward a more artistic direction with Rubber Soul, as with Revolver; they decide to stop touring
  7. 1967 Sgt Pepper's is released, often cited as the first concept album, and displaying innovative recording methods; The Beatles preform for the world's first worldwide TV program; the infamous visit to India and the death of Brian Epstein
  8. 1968-69 Launch of Apple Records label; Album sessions continue in studio, but each Beatle (save Ringo) seems to be diverging artistically and working more and more individually, and feel lost without Brian Epstein; last performance as a band on the roof of Apple Records
  9. 1970 The Beatles are announced to be officially split up
